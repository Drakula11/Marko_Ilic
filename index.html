TRAINING

Focused development, not generic workouts.

Every session is built around the athlete's current level and goals, with emphasis on technique, decision-making, movement and confidence.

01

Private training

1-on-1 work with highly specific technical corrections and a tailored plan.

  • Passing and shooting mechanics
  • Leg strength, balance and body position
  • Position-specific development
02

Small group

Competitive repetitions with more decisions, movement and game-like situations.

  • 2 to 6 athletes
  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Competitive technical drills
03

Performance prep

Targeted work for tryouts, camps, the upcoming season or higher-level competition.

  • Game-specific conditioning
  • Confidence and preparation
  • Individual development plan

ABOUT MARKO

Experience in Europe and the United States.

Marko Ilic is a water polo coach and former high-level athlete focused on technical growth, discipline and long-term player development.

As head coach of 18U teams at Channel Islands United, he worked on fundamentals, tactics and a culture of accountability and growth.

He has coached at camps across Texas and California, and in Serbia he led water polo and swimming programs for young athletes.

His private coaching combines technical detail, physical preparation and confidence-building in a demanding but supportive environment.
